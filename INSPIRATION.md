# Inspiration and clean-room boundary

Codex Mantle was informed by publicly visible workflows from three independent projects:

- [Codey](https://github.com/SuperGness/codey): an integrated desktop experience around Codex;
- [Codex++](https://github.com/xianyu110/CodexPlusPlus): desktop workflow enhancements;
- [OpenCodex](https://opencodex.me/zh-cn/guides/codex-integration/): provider and Codex integration ideas.

The Mantle implementation is clean-room. Contributors must not paste or mechanically translate code,
assets, schemas, or prose from those projects. Public behavior may motivate a requirement, but the
design, implementation, and tests in this repository must be independently produced. When a future
change intentionally incorporates third-party material, its source, version, license, and required
attribution must be recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` before merge.

The project name describes interoperability with OpenAI Codex. Codex Mantle is not affiliated with or
endorsed by OpenAI, and it must not use OpenAI trade dress or imply official status.
