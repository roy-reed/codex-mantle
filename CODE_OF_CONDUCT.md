# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Codex Mantle welcoming and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's credentials, identity, private workspace data,
  or communications.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and repair harm.
- Put the safety and health of the community ahead of individual advantage.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, discriminatory language,
trolling, sustained disruption, and retaliation against reporters are unacceptable. Publishing
private information without explicit permission is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project in
public or private spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a confidential report, use the repository's GitHub private vulnerability reporting form and
prefix the title with `[Conduct]`. This channel is reused only to provide a private maintainer contact;
conduct reports are not treated as software vulnerabilities. Include links or screenshots only when
necessary, and remove unrelated private information.

Maintainers will acknowledge reports privately when possible, limit access to people needed for the
response, investigate fairly, and avoid conflicts of interest. Proportionate consequences may include
a private correction, warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent ban. Retaliation
or knowingly false reports may receive the same consequences. Maintainers will explain public actions
when doing so does not compromise reporter privacy.
